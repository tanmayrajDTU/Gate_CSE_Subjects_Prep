TREND ANALYSIS · RISING & FALLING SUBJECTS

Subject Weight Trends: Period 1 (2007–2013) vs Period 2 (2014–2020) vs Period 3 (2021–2026)

📈 GAINING Subjects

↑ DSA — +40% weight since 2015

Graph algorithms, DP, and advanced trees dominate recent papers. Algorithmic thinking rewarded heavily.

↑ Discrete Mathematics — +25% since 2018

Graph theory, combinatorics, and logic questions merged with EngMath for comprehensive coverage.

↑ Theory of Computation — slowly gaining

Decidability and complexity class questions (P/NP) are increasingly favored post-2019.

↑ Probability/Statistics (within EngMath)

Bayesian probability, conditional expectations, and distributions increasingly tested.

📉 DECLINING Subjects

↓ Software Engineering — −60% since 2015

SDLC models, testing, UML mostly absent. Dropped from 5–6 marks to 0–2 marks. Near elimination.

↓ Digital Logic — −30% since 2016

Boolean simplification and K-maps less frequent. Circuit design rare post-2019.

↓ Computer Organization — −20% since 2017

Pipeline hazards still appear but less numerically intensive. Memory hierarchy reduced.

→ Programming Languages / Compilers — Stable but shifting

Parsing and CFG moved to TOC overlap. Runtime environments and type systems dropped.
